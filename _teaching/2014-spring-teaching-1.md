This course introduced students to the scientific methods psychologists use to investigate the relationship between the brain and behavior. The course emphasized the research process, with how scientific questions are developed, tested, analyzed, and communicated. Students learned the foundations of experimental design, hypothesis generation and testing, and evaluation of empirical research. A component of the course was a semester-long research project in which students moved through each stage of the process. From conducting literature reviews, designing a study, analyzing findings, and writing an APA-style research paper. Students gained experience with psychological science and strengthened their scientific writing and communication skills.
